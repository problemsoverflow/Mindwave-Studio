Mode 5: Mindwave State

Rapid Modulation Music

This mode is based on research showing that subtle, fast-changing sound patterns can align with the brain’s natural rhythmic activity. By matching these internal brain rhythms, the sound helps the brain “lock in” to a focused state instead of becoming distracted or understimulated. It is especially useful for maintaining attention during demanding tasks or for individuals who find it difficult to stay engaged with static audio.
