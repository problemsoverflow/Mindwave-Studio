Mode 4: Immersion

Ambient Sounds/Environment

This mode is designed around ambient noise tracks that create a consistent, surrounding sound environment. Rather than functioning as music, the audio acts as a steady auditory space that reduces awareness of external distractions and supports mental immersion. It is most effective for long focus sessions, deep work, or situations where environmental noise would otherwise break concentration.
