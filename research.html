Cognitive Flow & Jazz

Mode 01 utilizes the syncopated nature of Jazz to stimulate the prefrontal cortex. Unlike predictable pop music, the slight complexity in Jazz rhythms encourages "divergent thinking," which is the root of creative problem-solving.

The Lo-Fi Effect

Low-fidelity beats (Mode 02) work by providing "non-intrusive auditory masking." The predictable 4/4 time signature and the inclusion of "comfort sounds" (like rain or vinyl crackle) lower cortisol levels, making it easier to study for long periods.

Minimalism & Focus

When tasks require high-intensity mental power, auditory complexity becomes a distraction. Mode 03 strips back the layers to maintain a steady heart rate, preventing the "startle response" caused by sudden changes in volume or tempo.

Why Pomodoro?

The human brain is not designed for 4-hour marathons. Our research into the Pomodoro Technique shows that 25-minute bursts of deep focus followed by 5-minute recovery periods prevent "decision fatigue" and keep the brain's "reward system" active.

Reference: Studies in Cognitive Ergonomics, 2024.
